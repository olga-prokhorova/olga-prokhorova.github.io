OLGA PROKHOROVA

My practice is rooted in performance, but performance for me is not limited to the stage. I work with the body, everyday situations, public space, participation and the fragile space between performer and viewer.

I am interested in what happens when familiar situations are slightly shifted — when an ordinary place, gesture or encounter begins to feel different. My works often emerge from simple actions, repetition, attention and presence.

Questions of belonging, displacement, family, gender and personal dignity are present in my work. I approach them through poetic interventions and situations that leave room for uncertainty, vulnerability and different interpretations.

Alongside performance, I work with poetry and photography. These practices allow me to continue exploring the relationship between body, memory, personal narrative and image.
