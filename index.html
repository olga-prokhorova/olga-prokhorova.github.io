About

Olga Prokhorova is a performance artist whose practice has developed across Finland, Sweden, Russia, Norway and Mexico.

Her work combines performance art, movement, visual communication and social interaction. She has presented performances in festivals, galleries, public spaces and collaborative projects since 2009.
